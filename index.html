DEPARTMENT OF ELECTRONICS & INSTRUMENTATION

Precision Engineering, Transducer Research & Intelligent Automation

The Department of Electronics and Instrumentation has been a beacon of innovation in physical transducer design, industrial process control, embedded signal processing, and bio-metrology. SENSONICS serves as the annual national platform where budding engineers showcase real-time hardware solutions and logical breakthroughs.
